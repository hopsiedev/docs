<Update label="June 9, 2026" description="Launch week">

The Vellix Hosting documentation site is live. This release brings a full set of guides for managing your game server, scaling your plan, and securing your account.

### New features

**Self-serve plan upgrades and downgrades**

Scale your server's RAM, CPU, and disk at any time from the client area — no data loss, with pro-rated billing for the remainder of your cycle. See [Billing & Support Tickets](/getting-started/billing).

**Startup options and port allocations**

Switch Java versions, tweak startup variables, and request additional port allocations for plugins like Dynmap, Votifier, and Simple Voice Chat. See [Startup Options & Port Allocations](/management/server-settings).

**One-click MySQL databases**

Provision and manage MySQL/MariaDB databases for plugins like LuckPerms, CoreProtect, and Dynmap directly from the panel. See [MySQL Databases](/databases/mysql).

**Subusers with granular permissions**

Invite builders, developers, and co-owners to your panel with fine-grained per-feature permissions. See [Subusers & Permissions](/collaboration/subusers).

**Automatic and manual backups**

Create on-demand backups before risky changes, schedule recurring backups, and restore in a click. See [Backups & Restoration](/backups/backups).

**SFTP access**

Move modpacks, maps, and bulk files faster using any SFTP client (FileZilla, WinSCP). See [SFTP Connection](/management/sftp).

**Two-factor authentication**

Protect your account and server files with TOTP-based 2FA from the account settings page. See [Account Security](/getting-started/security).

### Updates

**Optimization playbook**

A new performance guide covers chunk pre-generation with Chunky, server config tuning, view-distance recommendations, and tips for keeping servers at a steady 20 TPS. See [Server Optimization & Performance Tips](/management/optimization).

**Mod and plugin installation workflow**

Step-by-step guidance for installing plugins, mods, and full modpacks, including version-compatibility checks across Spigot, Paper, Forge, Fabric, and NeoForge. See [Uploading & Installing Mods/Plugins](/customization/install-mods).

**Updated Discord support link**

The Discord community invite has been refreshed so you can reach support and other server owners faster.

</Update>
